## my-favorite-series
> **WandaVision**
> WandaVision is my favorite show of all time since it has my favorite character in it, Wanda.
> WandaVision, as written in the title is based on the "story" of Wanda Maximoff and Vision after the events of Avengers: Endgame.
> What's so interesting is that Vision died during Avengers: Infinity War so it was a shock to find that Vision is alive in this post-Endgame show.
> There's many more characters in this show like so:

1. **Wanda Maximoff** (*played by Elizabeth Olsen*)
2. **Vision** (*played by Paul Bettany*)
3. **Agatha Harkness/Agnes** (*played by Kathryn Hahn*)
4. **Monica Rambeau** (*played by Teyonah Paris*)
5. **Pietro Maximoff** (*played by Evan Peters*)
6. **Jimmy Woo** (*played by Randall Park*)
7. **Darcy Lewis** (*played by Kat Dennings*)

> And it's even crazier to say that my second all-time favorite show is **Agatha All Along** which is the successor to Wandavision following what happened to Agatha Harkness after the events of Wandavision and Doctor Strange: Multiverse of Madness.
